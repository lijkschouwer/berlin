# The Things

I want to talk about the things.

The things that are making me crawl against the walls inside my skull.

The things that I know you know but pretend that you don't, but maybe you're not pretending which leaves me with the thing where I have to talk about the things.

The thing where we pretend we are compassionate, but we're not.

The thing where you went to college and thought you would be rewarded, but most definitely were not.

The thing where you all lie in riddles, front in class at lower angle, snark in reddit, then frolic in jovial gallows. 

The thing where you're sunk in wallow, perched in pity, peached in pretty, marrow until hollowed.
